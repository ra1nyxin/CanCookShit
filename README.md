能煮屎 / CanCookShit

饥荒联机版模组，让粪肥和鸟粪成为可以直接食用和入锅的食材。

粪肥：基础饱食度 +8、理智值 -15。
鸟粪：基础饱食度 +6、理智值 -25。
粪肥入锅提供 0.5 肉度和 0.5 怪物度；鸟粪提供 0.5 怪物度和 0.5 乳制品度。两者可以放进冰箱和盐盒，也可以喂给其他玩家。
给猪王一份粪肥换 3 个金块，一份鸟粪换 5 个金块。
食用时有中文角色台词，检查台词沿用游戏原版。角色自身的食物效果仍按原版结算。
食用粪肥使体温恒定在 25 度 30 分钟，饥饿消耗减半 5 分钟；鸟粪分别为 5 分钟和 15 分钟。
两种效果分别计时；再次食用任一种会按该食物的时长重置计时，不叠加倍率。死亡时效果结束。
沃利的便携烹饪锅部署后也可以使用这两种食材。
喂皮弗娄牛可增加顺从、驯服、生命和饱食：粪肥分别为 5%、1.2%、6 和 10；鸟粪为 3%、1%、4 和 8。
游戏中按住 Shift 五秒会掉落一份粪肥，持续按住则每五秒掉落一份。
给猪人、鱼人或忠诚鱼人守卫喂粪肥或鸟粪可使其永久忠诚；鱼人被原版连带雇佣时也会永久忠诚。领导者死亡或离开后忠诚会结束。
手持粪肥或鸟粪对着未满耐久或未满使用次数的物品右键，可以用一份食材恢复目标最大值的一半；支持使用次数、燃料值和护甲耐久，包括亮茄盔甲。

服务器和所有客户端都需要启用此模组。

English

This Don't Starve Together mod makes manure and guano edible and usable as crock pot ingredients. Manure has base values of +8 hunger and -15 sanity; guano has +6 hunger and -25 sanity. Manure provides 0.5 meat and 0.5 monster value; guano provides 0.5 monster and 0.5 dairy value. Both can be stored in ice boxes and salt boxes or fed to other players. Eating dialogue is currently in Chinese; inspection dialogue remains unchanged. Character-specific food modifiers still apply.

The server and all clients must enable the mod.

Trade manure to the Pig King for 3 gold nuggets, or guano for 5 gold nuggets each.

Feeding a beefalo manure grants 5% obedience, 1.2% domestication, 6 health and 10 hunger; guano grants 3%, 1%, 4 and 8 respectively.

Manure keeps body temperature at 25 degrees for 30 minutes and halves hunger drain for 5 minutes. Guano keeps temperature stable for 5 minutes and halves hunger drain for 15 minutes. Eating either resets each timer to that food's duration; effects do not stack and end on death. Warly's deployed portable crock pot accepts both ingredients.

Hold Shift for five seconds to drop manure; keep holding to drop one every five seconds.

Feed manure or guano to pigs, merms, or merm guards for permanent loyalty, including merms recruited by the vanilla group effect. Loyalty ends when their leader dies or leaves.

With manure or guano in hand, right-click an item with missing durability or uses to restore half of its maximum with one piece. This supports finite uses, fuel, and armor durability, including Lunar Plant armor.
