能煮屎 / CanCookShit

饥荒联机版模组，让粪肥和鸟粪成为可以直接食用和入锅的食材。

粪肥：基础饱食度 +8、理智值 -15。
鸟粪：基础饱食度 +6、理智值 -25。
每份入锅提供 0.5 肉度和 0.5 怪物度，可以放进冰箱和盐盒，也可以喂给其他玩家。
给猪王一份粪肥换 3 个金块，一份鸟粪换 5 个金块。
食用时有中文角色台词，检查台词沿用游戏原版。角色自身的食物效果仍按原版结算。
食用粪肥使体温恒定在 25 度 30 分钟，饥饿消耗减半 5 分钟；鸟粪分别为 5 分钟和 15 分钟。
两种效果分别计时；再次食用任一种会按该食物的时长重置计时，不叠加倍率。死亡时效果结束。
沃利的便携烹饪锅部署后也可以使用这两种食材。
喂皮弗娄牛可增加顺从、驯服、生命和饱食：粪肥分别为 4%、1%、5 和 8；鸟粪为 2%、0.8%、3 和 6。
游戏中按住 Shift 五秒会掉落一份粪肥，持续按住则每五秒掉落一份。
给猪人粪肥可雇佣 2 小时，鸟粪可雇佣 4 小时（现实时间）。

服务器和所有客户端都需要启用此模组。

English

This Don't Starve Together mod makes manure and guano edible and usable as crock pot ingredients. Manure has base values of +8 hunger and -15 sanity; guano has +6 hunger and -25 sanity. Each provides 0.5 meat and 0.5 monster value. Both can be stored in ice boxes and salt boxes or fed to other players. Eating dialogue is currently in Chinese; inspection dialogue remains unchanged. Character-specific food modifiers still apply.

The server and all clients must enable the mod.

Trade manure to the Pig King for 3 gold nuggets, or guano for 5 gold nuggets each.

Feeding a beefalo manure grants 4% obedience, 1% domestication, 5 health and 8 hunger; guano grants 2%, 0.8%, 3 and 6 respectively.

Manure keeps body temperature at 25 degrees for 30 minutes and halves hunger drain for 5 minutes. Guano keeps temperature stable for 5 minutes and halves hunger drain for 15 minutes. Eating either resets each timer to that food's duration; effects do not stack and end on death. Warly's deployed portable crock pot accepts both ingredients.

Hold Shift for five seconds to drop manure; keep holding to drop one every five seconds.

Give manure to a pig to hire it for 2 real-time hours, or guano for 4 hours.
